# Product Descriptions

## Black Tea

### Ilam First Flush (HT-BLK-001)

Picked in early spring from gardens high in Ilam, this is the first harvest after the winter rest. The leaves are lightly oxidised, so the cup is pale gold with a floral, almost green character and a crisp finish. Drink it without milk. 100 g pouch.

### Ilam Second Flush Muscatel (HT-BLK-002)

Harvested in early summer, when the leaves develop the muscatel grape note that second flush teas are known for. Fuller and rounder than the first flush, with a coppery cup and hints of stone fruit. Good on its own or with a little milk. 100 g pouch.

### Dhankuta Autumn Flush (HT-BLK-003)

An autumn harvest from Dhankuta with a deep amber cup, malty body and notes of toasted nuts and dried fruit. Mellow and low in astringency. Stands up well to milk. 100 g pouch.

### Himalayan Breakfast Blend (HT-BLK-004)

Our everyday black tea: a blend of strong, malty Nepali black teas designed for milk and sugar, but smooth enough to drink plain. A good choice for beginners and for making chai at home. 100 g pouch.

### Golden Tips (HT-BLK-005)

Made only from young buds covered in fine golden down, picked and processed by hand in small batches. Honeyed and silky, with notes of cocoa and baked sweet potato and almost no bitterness. A tea for special occasions; drink it without milk. 50 g pouch.

## Green Tea

### Kanyam Spring Green (HT-GRN-001)

A pan-fired green tea from the Kanyam hills, picked in spring. The cup is bright and grassy, with a gentle nutty sweetness. Brew it cooler than black tea to keep it from turning bitter. 100 g pouch.

### Himalayan Steamed Green (HT-GRN-002)

Steamed rather than pan-fired, which keeps the leaves a vivid green and gives a fresh, vegetal flavour with a hint of the sea. Small-batch and often in short supply. 100 g pouch.

### Jasmine Green Pearls (HT-GRN-003)

Young green tea leaves rolled by hand into small pearls and scented over several nights with fresh jasmine blossoms. The pearls unfurl as they steep, giving a floral, perfumed cup. 100 g pouch.

## Oolong Tea

### Mist Valley Oolong (HT-OOL-001)

A lightly oxidised oolong from high gardens often wrapped in morning mist. Creamy, floral and smooth, with a lingering sweetness. Excellent for several short steeps. 100 g pouch.

### Roasted Hill Oolong (HT-OOL-002)

A darker oolong that is slowly roasted over charcoal after rolling. Warm notes of caramel, toasted grain and stone fruit, with a smooth, comforting body. 100 g pouch.

## White Tea

### Silver Needle (HT-WHT-001)

Made only from unopened buds, simply withered and dried. The cup is pale and delicate, with notes of melon, honey and fresh hay. Our rarest tea, produced in very small quantities each spring. 50 g pouch.

### Moonlight White (HT-WHT-002)

Buds and young leaves withered slowly in the shade, giving a white tea with more body than Silver Needle: soft, sweet and fruity, with a hint of apricot. 100 g pouch.

## Herbal Tea

### Tulsi Lemongrass Infusion (HT-HRB-001)

Holy basil (tulsi) and lemongrass from mid-hill farms, blended for a bright, citrusy and slightly peppery cup. Caffeine-free, and refreshing hot or iced. 100 g pouch.

### Himalayan Chamomile (HT-HRB-002)

Whole chamomile flowers with a soft, apple-like sweetness. Calming and caffeine-free; a favourite before bed. 100 g pouch.

### Ginger Turmeric Herbal (HT-HRB-003)

Dried ginger root, turmeric and a little black pepper, for a warming, spicy cup. Caffeine-free. Lovely with honey and a slice of lemon. 100 g pouch.

## Masala Chai

### Classic Masala Chai (HT-CHI-001)

Strong Nepali black tea blended with cardamom, cinnamon, ginger, cloves and black pepper. Made to be simmered with milk and sugar in the traditional way. 100 g pouch.

### Cardamom Chai (HT-CHI-002)

Black tea with a generous amount of green cardamom and nothing else, for a fragrant, sweet-smelling chai that is gentler than our classic blend. 100 g pouch.

## Teaware

### Clay Teapot (HT-WAR-001)

A hand-thrown clay teapot that holds about 500 ml. The unglazed clay keeps heat well and suits black and oolong teas. Rinse with hot water only; do not use soap, which the clay absorbs.

### Glass Gaiwan (HT-WAR-002)

A heat-resistant glass lidded bowl of about 150 ml, used for brewing short, repeated steeps of oolong, green and white teas. The clear glass lets you watch the leaves unfurl.

### Cast Iron Kettle (HT-WAR-003)

A traditional cast iron kettle of about 1 litre with an enamel-lined interior, for heating water on the stove. Not for use in a microwave or on an induction hob. Dry it thoroughly after each use.

### Bamboo Tea Scoop (HT-WAR-004)

A hand-carved bamboo scoop that holds about one serving of loose-leaf tea, roughly 2 to 3 grams.

### Stainless Steel Infuser (HT-WAR-005)

A fine-mesh stainless steel basket infuser that fits most mugs and teapots, with a lid that doubles as a drip tray. Dishwasher-safe.

## Gift Sets

### Himalayan Tasting Set (HT-GFT-001)

Five 25 g pouches in a gift box: Ilam First Flush, Ilam Second Flush Muscatel, Mist Valley Oolong, Kanyam Spring Green and Tulsi Lemongrass Infusion, with a card of brewing notes. A good introduction to Nepali tea and a popular gift.
