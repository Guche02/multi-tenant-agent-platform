# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Returning tea

You can return any tea within 14 days of delivery if the pouch is unopened and its seal is unbroken. Opened tea cannot be returned for reasons of hygiene, unless there is a quality problem (see below).

## Quality problems with tea

If a tea tastes stale, smells musty, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 14 days of delivery, even if you have opened it. We will replace it or refund it in full. A short description of the problem is enough; you do not need to send the tea back.

## Damaged or broken teaware

If teaware arrives cracked or broken, report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and the packaging. We will send a replacement at no cost, or give you a full refund if the item is out of stock. Please keep the damaged item until we confirm.

## Returning teaware you no longer want

Unused teaware in its original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. We arrange the pickup in the Kathmandu Valley; outside the valley, you send it back to us and we refund the courier cost along with the item.

## How refunds are paid

Because all orders are paid cash on delivery, we pay refunds by bank transfer or to a mobile wallet in your name. Refunds are paid within 7 business days of us approving the return. We refund the price you paid for the item, not its current price.

## Cancelling instead of returning

An order that is still pending can simply be cancelled, with nothing to pay. Once an order is confirmed it cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse it at the door or return it under this policy.

## Items that cannot be returned

Opened tea without a quality problem, and teaware that has been used, cannot be returned. Gift sets can be returned only if every pouch in the set is unopened.
