# Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering

### How do I place an order?

Tell the chat assistant which teas or teaware you would like and how many. It will show you a summary with the current prices and the total, and place the order once you confirm. You need to be logged in to order.

### Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, while the order is still pending. You can change the items, the quantities or the delivery address, or cancel it, by asking the chat assistant. Once we confirm an order (usually within one business day) it can no longer be changed or cancelled.

### Can I order the same thing as last time?

Yes. Ask the assistant to repeat a previous order. It will check that everything is still in stock and show you today's prices before placing the new order, since prices and availability may have changed.

### Is there a minimum or maximum order?

There is no minimum. You can order as many of an item as we have in stock; the assistant will tell you if we have fewer than you asked for and suggest an alternative.

### What happens if an item is out of stock?

The assistant will tell you and suggest a similar tea or item that is available. Many of our teas are seasonal, so a tea that is out of stock usually returns after the next harvest.

## Prices and payment

### How much does a tea cost?

Prices change with each harvest, so they are not listed in these articles. Ask the chat assistant for the current price of any product; it always shows live prices.

### How can I pay?

We accept cash on delivery only. You pay the courier when your order arrives.

### Do you charge for delivery?

No. Delivery is free anywhere in Nepal, with no minimum order.

## Delivery

### How long does delivery take?

1 to 2 business days in the Kathmandu Valley, 3 to 5 business days to other cities, and 5 to 8 business days to remote and hill districts, counted from when the order is confirmed.

### Do you ship outside Nepal?

Not at the moment. We deliver only to addresses in Nepal.

### How do I track my order?

Ask the chat assistant. It shows each order's current status, items, total and status history.

## Tea and brewing

### Which tea should a beginner try?

The Himalayan Breakfast Blend is a smooth, forgiving black tea that works with or without milk. For something lighter, try Kanyam Spring Green. If you want to explore, the Himalayan Tasting Set has five of our teas in small pouches.

### Which teas have no caffeine?

Our herbal infusions are naturally caffeine-free: Tulsi Lemongrass Infusion, Himalayan Chamomile and Ginger Turmeric Herbal. White and green teas have less caffeine than black tea, but none of our true teas are caffeine-free.

### How should I store tea?

Keep it in its resealable pouch or an airtight tin, away from light, heat, moisture and strong smells such as spices or coffee. Do not keep tea in the fridge. Stored this way, most teas stay fresh for about a year; green teas are best within six months.

### Can I re-steep the leaves?

Yes. Oolong, white and green teas can be steeped two to four times, and each steep tastes a little different. Black teas usually give one strong cup and one lighter second steep.

## Returns

### Can I return tea I don't like?

You can return unopened tea within 14 days of delivery. Opened tea can only be returned if there is a quality problem, such as stale or musty tea.

### What if my teapot arrives broken?

Report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo, and we will replace it or refund it in full.

### How long do refunds take?

Refunds are paid by bank transfer or to a mobile wallet within 7 business days of us approving the return.
