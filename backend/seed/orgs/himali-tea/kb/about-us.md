# About Himali Tea House

## Our story

Himali Tea House began in 2014 as a small tasting room in Kathmandu, run by a family that had picked and processed tea in the eastern hills of Nepal for three generations. Customers kept asking where they could buy the teas they had tasted, so we started packing them to order. Today we are an online store that ships fresh Nepali tea and teaware to homes across Nepal.

## Where our tea comes from

Almost all of our tea is grown in eastern Nepal, mainly in Ilam, Dhankuta and Kanyam, at altitudes between 1,200 and 2,200 metres. The cool mountain air and slow growth give the leaves a bright, layered flavour. We buy directly from small family gardens and cooperatives we have known for years, and we visit them every season. Our herbal infusions use tulsi, lemongrass, chamomile, ginger and turmeric grown by farmers' groups in the mid-hills.

## Freshness

We buy in small lots after each harvest and pack our teas in resealable, light-proof pouches. Each pouch is marked with its harvest season. We would rather run out of a tea than sell one that has gone stale, so some teas are seasonal and may be out of stock between harvests.

## What we sell

We sell loose-leaf black, green, oolong and white teas, herbal infusions and masala chai blends, along with teaware for brewing them at home and a tasting set for gifts. Every tea comes in a 100 g pouch unless its description says otherwise.
