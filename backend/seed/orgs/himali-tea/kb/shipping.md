# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Where we deliver

We deliver to any address in Nepal. We do not ship outside Nepal at the moment.

## Delivery cost

Delivery is free on every order, anywhere in Nepal. There is no minimum order value.

## Payment

We accept cash on delivery only. Please have the exact amount ready if you can; our couriers carry limited change. Card and wallet payments are not available yet.

## Order stages

Every order moves through four stages:

1. **Pending**: we have received your order but not yet confirmed it. You can still change the items, the quantities or the delivery address, or cancel the order.
2. **Confirmed**: we have checked your order and are packing it. From this point the order can no longer be changed or cancelled.
3. **Shipped**: your parcel is with the courier.
4. **Delivered**: the courier has handed over your parcel.

We usually confirm orders within one business day. If you need to change something after an order is confirmed, contact us and we will see what we can do, but we cannot guarantee a change.

## Delivery times

- **Kathmandu Valley** (Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur): 1 to 2 business days after the order is confirmed.
- **Other cities** such as Pokhara, Biratnagar, Butwal and Dharan: 3 to 5 business days.
- **Remote and hill districts**: 5 to 8 business days, and sometimes longer in the monsoon or during festivals.

Business days are Sunday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## Tracking your order

Ask the chat assistant for the status of any of your orders. It shows the current stage, the items, the total and the history of status changes.

## Failed deliveries

If the courier cannot reach you, they will try again the next business day. After two failed attempts the parcel comes back to us and the order is cancelled. You are welcome to place it again.
