# Service Descriptions

## Massage

### Swedish Massage

A relaxing full-body massage using long, flowing strokes, gentle kneading and light pressure to ease tension and improve circulation. A good first massage and ideal for stress relief. Offered by Anjali Shrestha and Maya Gurung.

### Deep Tissue Massage

Slower, firmer pressure that works into the deeper layers of muscle to release chronic tightness and knots, especially in the back, neck and shoulders. You may feel some soreness for a day or two afterwards. Not recommended in the first 48 hours after an injury. Offered by Anjali Shrestha and Rohan Karki.

### Hot Stone Massage

Smooth, heated basalt stones are placed on key points and used as an extension of the therapist's hands. The warmth relaxes muscles so the massage can go deeper with less pressure. Our longest massage. Not suitable during pregnancy or for people with diabetes-related nerve damage. Offered by Anjali Shrestha.

## Physiotherapy

### Physiotherapy Assessment

Your first physiotherapy session. Your physiotherapist takes a full history, examines how you move, identifies the cause of your pain or injury, and agrees a treatment plan with you. The session includes initial treatment and exercises to start at home. Please bring any scans, X-rays or doctors' notes. Offered by Rohan Karki.

### Physiotherapy Follow-up

Shorter sessions after your assessment, to continue treatment, check your progress and adjust your exercises. Follow-ups are meant for clients who have already had a Physiotherapy Assessment, so if you are new to us, start with an assessment. Offered by Rohan Karki.

### Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Focused rehabilitation for sprains, strains, tendon problems and recovery after sports injuries or surgery, combining hands-on treatment with strength and mobility exercises to get you back to your sport safely. Offered by Rohan Karki.

## Yoga

### Private Yoga Session

A one-to-one yoga class planned around your goals and level, whether you are a complete beginner, returning after a break, or want to improve flexibility, strength or breathing. Mats and props are provided. Offered by Maya Gurung.

### Prenatal Yoga

Gentle, one-to-one yoga adapted for pregnancy, focusing on breathing, posture, pelvic floor awareness and relaxation. Suitable from the second trimester onwards, with your doctor's approval. Offered by Maya Gurung.
