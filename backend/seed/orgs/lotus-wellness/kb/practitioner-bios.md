# Practitioner Bios

## Anjali Shrestha

Anjali is our senior massage therapist and has practised for over twelve years. She trained in Swedish and deep tissue massage in Kathmandu and later in hot stone therapy in Bangkok. Clients describe her as calm and attentive; she is especially good at relieving neck and shoulder tension from desk work. Anjali offers Swedish Massage, Deep Tissue Massage and Hot Stone Massage.

## Rohan Karki

Rohan is a registered physiotherapist with a bachelor's degree in physiotherapy and eight years of experience, including four years with a national football academy. He specialises in sports injuries, back and knee pain, and recovery after surgery. Rohan offers Physiotherapy Assessment, Physiotherapy Follow-up and Sports Injury Rehabilitation, and also Deep Tissue Massage for clients whose recovery benefits from it.

## Maya Gurung

Maya is a certified yoga teacher with more than 500 hours of training and a specialist qualification in prenatal yoga. She has taught for nine years and is known for patient, beginner-friendly classes. Maya is also trained in Swedish massage. She offers Private Yoga Session, Prenatal Yoga and Swedish Massage.

## Choosing a practitioner

Some services are offered by more than one practitioner. When you book, you can ask for a particular practitioner, or take whoever is free at the time you want. All of our practitioners speak Nepali and English.
