# About Lotus Wellness Studio

## Who we are

Lotus Wellness Studio is a small massage, physiotherapy and yoga studio in Jhamsikhel, Lalitpur, founded in 2018. We believe that looking after your body should feel calm and personal, so every session is one-to-one with a single practitioner, in a private room.

## What we offer

We offer three kinds of care under one roof: therapeutic massage, physiotherapy for pain and injuries, and private yoga, including prenatal yoga. Our practitioners work together, so a physiotherapist can suggest a massage or a yoga session as part of your recovery.

## Our team

Our three practitioners are Anjali Shrestha, our senior massage therapist; Rohan Karki, our physiotherapist; and Maya Gurung, our yoga teacher, who is also trained in Swedish massage. Their full profiles are in our practitioner bios.

## Location and getting here

We are on the first floor of a quiet building in Jhamsikhel, Lalitpur, a short walk from the main Jhamsikhel road. There is space for scooters and bicycles in front of the building; car parking nearby is limited. The building has a ramp and a lift.

## Opening hours

We are open six days a week and closed on Saturdays, Nepal's weekly holiday, and on some public holidays. Hours vary by day, so ask the chat assistant for this week's opening hours and any upcoming holiday closures. All times are Nepal time.

## Booking and contact

The chat assistant can tell you about our services, find free times, and book, reschedule or cancel your sessions. You can also email hello@lotuswellness.example; we reply within one working day.
