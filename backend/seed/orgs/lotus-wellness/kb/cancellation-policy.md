# Cancellation and Late Arrival Policy

## Why we have this policy

Each session reserves a practitioner and a room just for you. When a session is cancelled at short notice, we usually cannot offer that time to anyone else. These rules keep things fair for everyone.

## Cancellation fees

The fee depends on how long before the session starts you cancel:

- **24 hours or more before**: free, no fee.
- **Between 2 and 24 hours before**: a fee of 50% of the session price.
- **Less than 2 hours before, or not turning up**: the full session price.

The fee is based on the price of the session when you booked it.

## How to cancel

Ask the chat assistant to cancel your booking. It will tell you whether a fee applies and how much before cancelling. You can also email us, but the cancellation counts from when we receive your email.

## Rescheduling

You can move a booking to another free time, free of charge, up to 24 hours before it starts. Within 24 hours of the session, bookings cannot be rescheduled; you can keep the booking or cancel it under the cancellation fees above. A new time must be free for your practitioner and must not overlap another of your bookings.

## Arriving late

Please arrive 5 to 10 minutes early. If you arrive late, your session still ends at its scheduled time, so that the next client is not delayed, and the full price applies. If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may not be able to start the session, and it is treated as a no-show.

## When we cancel

If we ever have to cancel your session, for example because a practitioner is ill, there is never a fee, and we will help you find a new time.

## Booking ahead

You can book up to 30 days ahead. Bookings must be made at least 2 hours before the session starts.

## Paying fees

Session prices and any cancellation fees are paid at the studio, in cash or by card, at your next visit.
