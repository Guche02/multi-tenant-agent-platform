# Frequently Asked Questions

## Booking

### How do I book a session?

Tell the chat assistant which service you want and when. It will find free times, optionally with a particular practitioner, and confirm the service, practitioner, date, time and price with you before booking. You need to be logged in to book.

### How far ahead can I book?

Up to 30 days ahead, and at least 2 hours before the session starts.

### Can I choose my practitioner?

Yes, for services that more than one practitioner offers. If you have no preference, the assistant will offer times with whoever is free.

### Can I book two sessions back to back?

Yes, as long as they do not overlap. For example, you can book a massage straight after a physiotherapy session. You cannot hold two bookings at the same time.

### Do you offer group classes?

No. All of our sessions, including yoga, are one-to-one with a single practitioner.

## Changes and cancellations

### How do I cancel?

Ask the chat assistant. Cancelling 24 hours or more before your session is free. Between 2 and 24 hours before, the fee is 50% of the session price. Less than 2 hours before, or not turning up, costs the full price.

### Can I reschedule?

Yes, free of charge, up to 24 hours before your session, as long as the new time is free. Within 24 hours of the session it can no longer be rescheduled.

### What happens if I'm late?

Your session still ends on time and the full price applies. If you are more than 15 minutes late, the session may be treated as a no-show.

## Prices and payment

### How much does a session cost?

Each service has its own price, which can change. Ask the chat assistant for current prices and durations.

### How do I pay?

You pay at the studio after your session, in cash or by card. We do not take payment online.

### Is physiotherapy covered by insurance?

Some health insurance plans cover physiotherapy. We can give you an itemised receipt to claim from your insurer, but we do not bill insurers directly.

## Visiting the studio

### When are you open?

We are closed on Saturdays and some public holidays, and hours vary between days. Ask the chat assistant for this week's opening hours and upcoming closures.

### What should I wear or bring?

For yoga, wear comfortable clothes you can move in; we provide mats and props. For massage, we provide towels and a private room to change in. For physiotherapy, wear loose clothing that lets us see the area being treated, and bring any scans or doctors' notes.

### Is there parking?

There is space for scooters and bicycles in front of the building. Car parking nearby is limited, so allow extra time if you are driving.

## Health and safety

### Can I have a massage while pregnant?

We offer Prenatal Yoga from the second trimester. For massage during pregnancy, please speak to your doctor first and tell us when booking; Hot Stone Massage is not suitable during pregnancy.

### Do I need a doctor's referral for physiotherapy?

No referral is needed. Start with a Physiotherapy Assessment, and bring any scans or notes you already have.

### What if I feel unwell on the day?

Please do not come in if you have a fever or a contagious illness. Let us know as early as you can; our usual cancellation fees apply, but we can waive them if you send a doctor's note.
